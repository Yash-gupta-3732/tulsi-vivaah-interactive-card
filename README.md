# 🪔 Surya Kumar and Sons Jewellers — 3D Tulsi Vivah & Dev Uthani Ekadashi Interactive Experience

An opulent, cinematic, real-time 3D interactive festive greeting card celebrating **Dev Uthani Ekadashi (देवउठनी एकादशी)** and **Tulsi Vivah (तुलसी विवाह)** for **Surya Kumar and Sons Jewellers (ज्वेलर्स सूर्य कुमार एण्ड संस)**.

Crafted with modern WebGL, Three.js, GSAP 3, and synthesized sacred temple audio via the Web Audio API.

## ✨ Features & Highlights

### 1. 🪔 Devotional 3D Visuals & Shrines
- **Sacred Tulsi Vrindavan**: Procedural 3D stone altar draped in festive silk fabrics with holy basil leaves.
- **Sugarcane Mandap (गन्ने का मंडप)**: Traditional 4-pillar sugarcane canopy adorned with marigold garlands.
- **Shaligram Shila & Sacred Chauki**: Reverently placed beside Mata Tulsi.
- **Traditional Brass Diyas**: Dynamic lighting with realistic flickering flames and soft shadows.
- **Floating 3D Petals & Stardust**: Real-time tumbling rose and marigold petals drifting in 3D space.
- **Hanging Temple Bells**: Realistic pendulum physics with manual ring action.

### 2. 🎵 Synthesized Sacred Temple Audio Engine
- Built 100% offline using the native **Web Audio API** (zero external mp3/audio files needed).
- **Tanpura Drone in C#** (Sa-Pa-Sa harmonic frequencies).
- **Acoustic Temple Bell Chimes**: Pure multi-harmonic brass resonance with natural exponential decay.

### 3. 🕊️ Interactive Ceremonial Dock
- **🔔 घंटी बजाएं**: Ring the sacred hanging brass bell with physical sway and resonant chiming.
- **🪔 दीप प्रज्वलन**: Illuminate and intensify the sacred diyas and warm candlelight.
- **🌸 पुष्प वर्षा**: Trigger an auspicious shower of fresh rose and marigold petals.
- **🌿 तुलसी अर्चन**: Activate the golden divine aura around Mata Tulsi.
- **360° Freeform Orbit**: Drag with mouse or swipe on mobile to orbit and explore the shrine in full 3D.
- **Chapter Stepper**: Jump smoothly across scenes (प्रवेश, वृंदावन, विवाह, शुभकामना, शुभेच्छु).

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Three.js (r128)**: Real-time 3D rendering, procedural geometries, PBR materials, point lights.
- **Three.js OrbitControls**: Fluid 3D camera navigation and mobile touch gestures.
- **GSAP 3 (3.12.5)**: High-performance cinematic camera animations and UI transitions.
- **Web Audio API**: Real-time harmonic synthesizer for Indian classical Tanpura drone and bell chimes.
- **CSS3 Glassmorphism**: Deep maroon and antique gold luxury design system with backdrop blur.
